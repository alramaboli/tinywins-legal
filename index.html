Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

TinyWins is designed to help you build habits while keeping your habit records on your device. The app does not require an account to track habits.

Who we are

TinyWins is published by Lehopotseng Ramaboli. For questions about this policy or your privacy, email alramaboli@gmail.com.

Information stored by TinyWins

To provide habit-tracking features, TinyWins stores the following information on your device:

  • Habit names and optional notes or descriptions.
  • Habit categories, selected icons and scheduled weekdays.
  • Dates when habits are created or updated, and whether they are archived.
  • Habit completion dates and times.
  • Whether you have completed onboarding.

Habit names and notes are entered by you. Please avoid adding sensitive information unless you are comfortable keeping it on your device. TinyWins does not offer a separate medical-record or health-measurement feature.

The app uses your device's date and time to record habit activity and calculate progress. It also assigns local identifiers to habit records.

Where your information is stored

Habit information is stored locally in a SQLite database within TinyWins' app storage on your device. TinyWins does not require an account or cloud synchronization, and habit tracking works offline. The reviewed app implementation does not upload habit records to a TinyWins server.

Google Play, your device's operating system and any enabled device backup services may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

Analytics, advertising and third-party services

The reviewed version of TinyWins does not integrate advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not provide account registration or login. TinyWins uses Expo, React Native and supporting software components to run the app and store data locally. The data practices of software components included in the distributed app must remain consistent with this policy.

Permissions

The reviewed TinyWins features do not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone or photo library. The Android app includes Internet permission, which allows network access but does not by itself mean habit records are uploaded.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Habit records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's data. You can:

  • Delete an individual habit to remove that habit and its recorded completion history.
  • Use Settings → Reset all data to remove habits, completion records and onboarding state from the app database.
  • Remove the app's stored data through your device's application settings.

Archiving a habit hides or marks it as archived; it does not delete the habit. TinyWins has no user account or server-side profile to delete. Deleted information may still exist in device backups, if backups were enabled, and deletion does not guarantee secure overwriting of physical storage.

Device backups

TinyWins is designed to keep your habit information on the device where you create it. The Android application is configured to exclude its data from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. TinyWins does not provide cloud synchronization or an account-based recovery service. If you uninstall the app, clear its data, lose your device, or switch to another device, your habit information and completion history may be permanently lost. This does not affect any copies that may have been created by earlier app versions or external device-management tools.

Security

TinyWins stores its local database within the app's storage area, which is protected by the operating system's app isolation mechanisms. The reviewed app does not add separate database-level encryption. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping its operating system updated. No storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Children

TinyWins is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when TinyWins features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this page, with its update date shown above.
